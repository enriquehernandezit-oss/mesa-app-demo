## What's Working

1. **The rank-flow back-guard.** Intercepting the platform BACK to unwind local flow state, with `beforeUnload` for reload/close and a `placedStamp` gate so success navigation passes through. Verified stepping one level per press. The kind of detail most apps ship broken.
2. **Reversible destructive actions.** "Quitar" optimistically removes, shows "Deshacer" for 5s, defers the DELETE, and on undo restores the exact object with note/tags intact. Frictionless and safe — better than a confirm dialog.
3. **Product-thesis-teaching microcopy.** "Tu respuesta mueve a {place}, no la puntuación del spot" at compare and reveal; the coachmark leads with "Sin estrellas. Solo comparas." Every score is attributed to a person. The no-ratings thesis is taught inline, not asserted.

## Priority Issues (all polish — no P0/P1)

### [P2] The restaurant-profile photo controls don't *guarantee* contrast on a bright cover
`.resto-back`/`.resto-share` now use the light on-photo glyph tokens (fixing the dark-photo case), but their scrim is only 55% opaque (`--photo-scrim-3`). B measured, over the demo's *light* placeholder gradient, back ← = 3.88:1 and share ↗ = **2.84:1 (fails the 3:1 non-text threshold)**. Real cover photos are usually dark (both glyphs were visually legible, and Candlelit is fully clean), so this bites only on a bright image — but the scrim should guarantee it regardless. **Fix:** deepen the button scrim (e.g. `--photo-scrim-3` 0.55 → ~0.7) or give the buttons a slightly stronger solid backing. `screens/restaurant/restaurant.css`.

### [P2] `.topbar__btn` "Compartir perfil" leaks the UA button-face grey
On `/profile` the share ↗ button renders on a solid `rgb(107,107,107)` background (Candlelit; a light grey in Afternoon) because `.topbar__btn` sets no `background` and the global `button{}` reset doesn't either — so the one topbar control that's a real `<button>` (not an `<a>`) falls through to `buttonface`. B measured 3.4:1. This has persisted across every prior critique. **Fix (one line):** `background: transparent` on `.topbar__btn`. `components/topbar.css`.

### [P2] Duplicate primary action on the restaurant profile
"Rankear otra vez" (outline, top) and the fixed "Rankear este spot" (filled, bottom) do the same thing; the fixed bar also visually clips the PUNTUACIONES score-circle labels beneath it (no scrim). Two competing entries dilute the one-CTA discipline. **Fix:** drop the top "Rankear otra vez" (keep the saved/checkmark control) and add a bottom fade so scrolling content doesn't collide with the bar. `screens/restaurant/RestaurantProfile.tsx`.

### [P3] Explore's two stacked chip rails are low-discoverability
Both rails scroll horizontally with off-edge options and no scroll affordance. **Fix:** an edge fade/peek on both rails, or collapse sectors behind a single "Sector ▾" chip that opens a sheet. `screens/explore/ExploreScreen.tsx`.

### [P3] Inert Reservar / Pedir / Unirme look tappable but do nothing
`aria-disabled`/`data-stale` is right for assistive tech, but a sighted first-timer tapping gets zero feedback and may read the app as broken. **Fix:** a subtle "pronto" tag or a one-line toast on tap. `components/QuickActions.tsx`.
