## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Not AI-generated — emphatically. Oxblood/brass palette, Cormorant numerals, film-grain photos, mono spec-sheet metadata = authored POV. No gradient text, hero-metric template, identical card grid, or glassmorphism. Per-section eyebrows are a committed brand system, not reflexive scaffolding.

Deterministic scan — 4 warnings:
- 1 genuine: PullToRefresh.tsx:50 animates `height` inline in TSX (Pass 6 grepped CSS only). Layout thrash on feed pull gesture.
- 3 false positives: bounce-easing on feed.css:169 (cheers-pop), screens.css:182 (stamp-in), tokens.css:135 (token def). These are the two celebration springs deliberately preserved in Pass 6.

Visual overlays: not attempted; used direct browser measurement instead.

## Overall Impression

Craft is real; the ranking flow is genuinely well-designed. But the product's own thesis doesn't fire. CLAUDE.md defines the atomic unit as "rank a place → see where friends rank theirs." Completed the loop end-to-end; the second half never happened. At the 9.6 reveal the screen shows only the user's own list, with a viewport-sized dead zone where the friend payoff belongs.

Biggest opportunity: put friends on the reveal screen.

## What's Working

1. The comparison flow is exemplary — "1 of 4" progress, misconception-killing subtitle, "new to your list" vs "#8 on your list" context, "About the same" escape.
2. The design language is a genuine moat. Do not restyle.
3. Domain voice excellent where it commits — the note placeholder teaches format by example.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Core loop payoff missing at peak moment
Reveal is a solo scoreboard in a social app, with a dead zone below the neighbors card. Nothing after posting shows friends either.
Fix: friends strip on .rank-reveal — "3 friends ranked this · avg 8.4" + 2-3 avatar/score rows. Data exists in /restaurants/:id friendsRankings. Fallback: "None of your friends have ranked this yet — you're first."
Command: /impeccable craft friends-on-reveal

### [P1] "Skip" posts the ranking — and can double-post
RankAPlace.tsx:260 (Skip) and :334 (Post ranking) call the same save.mutate(position). Skip means "don't do it" to users. Line 260 lacks disabled={save.isPending} while 334 has it → double-tap = two POSTs into a unique (userId, restaurantId) constraint. Reveal also shows "#1 of 43" before saving.
Fix: relabel "Post without a note", add isPending guard, commit at reveal or label reveal as preview.
Command: /impeccable clarify rank-flow-actions

### [P1] Ranking a place doesn't remove it from Want to try
routes/rankings.ts never touches savedPlaces (verified). Want-to-try decays into noise.
Fix: delete matching savedPlaces row on successful POST /rankings; invalidate ['saved'].
Command: /impeccable harden rankings-saved-sync

### [P1] Score badge covers the score (regression introduced in Pass 2)
Measured: badge covered 18% of the 34px circle at original 7px; 32% at current 10px. Badge widths 38–62px on a 34px circle.
Fix: on .scorebadge__circle--sm move attribution to caption below circle, or truncate to first name + max-width. Do not revert to 7px.
Command: /impeccable layout scorebadge-sm

### [P2] Language has no strategy
English shell with unpredictable Spanish. "‹ BACK" above "¿Cómo estuvo?" on one screen. Not i18n — drift.
Fix: pick one (Spanish-first defensible for the audience), document in DESIGN.md, sweep.
Command: /impeccable clarify language-strategy

## Persona Red Flags

Casey (distracted mobile): abandoning mid-rank loses everything silently. No draft, no resume, and the reveal looks saved when it isn't — the exact screen where interruption is most likely.

Jordan (first-timer): /rank right column mixes "not ranked" and a bare score circle with no legend — reads 8.7 as the restaurant's rating, inverting the no-stars premise. 7 filter chips at the first decision point; "Reserve" as a filter is unclear.

Riley (stress tester): Want-to-try desync; double-post on Skip; grey Share button — .topbar__btn never sets background and global.css doesn't reset button background, so Chrome UA buttonface grey rgb(107,107,107) leaks on the one .topbar__btn that is a <button> while its <a> sibling stays transparent.

## Minor Observations

- Rankings rows 153–234px, ~3.5/screen; 43 entries ≈ 13 screens. Names wrap at 375px (thumb 56 + score 40 squeeze middle column).
- Duplicate IA: Profile "Ranked 43 ›" / "Want to try ›" duplicate Rankings chips; "Rankings" vs "Ranked" naming.
- Duplicate stats: "Rank in DR #1" twice on Profile; streak "5W 🔥" vs "5 weeks".
- Cognitive load 2/8 failures (moderate): minimal-choices (7-chip rail), working-memory (alphabetical list, friend context not carried forward).
- "#1 of 43 on your list" on reveal is a <button> with no handler — false affordance.
- "ADD A DISH" header labels an "Add a photo" control.
- ErrorState suggests "Pull to refresh" on Explore/Map which aren't pull-to-refresh; no retry button.
- "avg 8.4" near-meaningless (own list mean is always centered).
- "No me convenció" de-emphasized via lower contrast → nudges away from negative ratings, inflates scores.
